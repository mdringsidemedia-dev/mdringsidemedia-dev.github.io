What’s Next for Damian Priest?

After tensions with R-Truth, where does Damian Priest go from here? A potential Finn Bálor feud and a move to Raw could give him a route back towards the main-event picture.

By MD Ringside Media · 16 October 2026
MD RINGSIDE MEDIA PRESENTSWHAT’S NEXT
FOR DAMIAN PRIEST?WWE · OPINION & PREDICTIONS

For a while, Damian Priest was in a tag team with R-Truth and seemed stuck in the classic tag-team loop. But on the September 11 episode of SmackDown, he finally had enough, snapped, and attacked R-Truth. The tag-team run was not too bad; they even became tag team champions before losing the titles to the MFTs after a long feud.

WWE now appears to be building a rivalry between R-Truth and Priest. In vignettes, Priest has looked increasingly menacing, and last week on SmackDown, R-Truth attacked Priest during a backstage segment. So, what comes next for Priest?

Priest needs to win the next chapter

Most likely, Priest and R-Truth will eventually settle their business in the ring on SmackDown. I believe WWE could turn this into a squash match, with Priest beating R-Truth in a very short time. If that does not happen, honestly, I struggle to see a clear direction for Priest in the company. He needs a decisive win to move forward.

If Priest wins — which I think he should — I can see two possible paths for him.

Could Finn Bálor be next?

First, I could definitely see Priest entering a feud with Finn Bálor, something WWE has teased in the past when Bálor first joined SmackDown. It could make sense given the hints after Bálor pinned Priest in a fatal four-way match, with the winner earning a place in a number-one-contender match against Sami Zayn.

After Bálor won and celebrated, the two shared one final handshake after the show. Was that the end of their story, or could there still be another chapter? Only time will tell.

A move to Raw could open the door

I think the best move for Priest would be to go to Raw. I do not really see a place for him on SmackDown unless he enters the United States Championship picture, which I am not convinced WWE will do. SmackDown's world-title scene has plenty of contenders, and I struggle to see where Priest fits in the near future.

Meanwhile, the World Heavyweight Championship picture on Raw could use fresh challengers. If Priest moved to Raw, I would want him to settle things once and for all with Dominik Mysterio first. That could build momentum and potentially lead to Priest challenging Roman Reigns for the World Heavyweight Championship.

My verdict: Priest needs a convincing win over R-Truth, followed by a storyline that feels meaningful. A renewed rivalry with Finn Bálor or a move to Raw could give him a clearer path back towards the main event.
